Mechanic Speak

Wheel bearing

The set of steel balls or rollers that lets each wheel spin smoothly.

In plain English

A wheel bearing is a ring of hardened steel balls or rollers that sits in the hub behind each wheel. It lets the wheel spin freely with very little friction while carrying the weight of the car. On most modern cars it is a sealed unit, often sold as a complete hub assembly.

Why it's on your estimate

When a bearing wears, it makes a humming, growling, or roaring noise that gets louder with speed and often changes when you steer left or right.

How urgent is it?

Fix soon

A noisy bearing gets worse over time. Left long enough it can overheat or develop enough looseness to affect braking and steering, so plan the repair rather than ignoring it.

Questions to ask the shop

  • Which wheel is it, and how did you confirm that?
  • Is it a pressed-in bearing or a whole hub assembly?
