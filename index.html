Mechanic Speak

What did the mechanic just say?

Look up the word on your repair estimate. Each page tells you what the part does, why a shop recommends the work, how urgent it usually is, and what to ask.

  • AlternatorThe engine-driven generator that makes electricity for the car and keeps the battery charged.
  • Brake caliperThe clamp at each wheel that squeezes the brake pads against the spinning disc to stop the car.
  • Brake rotor resurfacingShaving a thin layer off the brake discs so the pads grip a smooth, flat surface.
  • Catalytic converterA part in the exhaust that turns the worst engine fumes into less harmful gases.
  • Control armA hinged metal link that holds a wheel to the car's frame and lets it move up and down.
  • Coolant flushDraining the old engine coolant and refilling with fresh.
  • CV axleThe shaft that carries power from the transmission to a wheel while letting it steer and bounce.
  • Oxygen (O2) sensorA sensor in the exhaust that tells the engine computer whether it's burning too much or too little fuel.
  • Serpentine beltOne long rubber belt that the engine spins to power things like the alternator and A/C.
  • StrutsThe combined spring-and-shock units that hold the car up and keep it from bouncing.
  • Throttle body cleaningCleaning the valve that controls how much air goes into the engine.
  • Tie rod endThe small pivoting joint that connects your steering to each front wheel.
  • Timing beltA toothed belt that keeps the engine's valves and pistons moving in step.
  • Wheel bearingThe set of steel balls or rollers that lets each wheel spin smoothly.
General information to help you talk with your mechanic. It is not a diagnosis of your car.
