Mechanic Speak

CV axle

The shaft that carries power from the transmission to a wheel while letting it steer and bounce.

In plain English

A CV axle (constant velocity axle) is a metal shaft with a flexible joint at each end. It carries the engine's power out to a wheel while still letting that wheel turn left and right and move up and down over bumps. Each joint is packed with grease and sealed inside a rubber cover called a boot.

Why it's on your estimate

The usual story is a torn boot: the grease leaks out, dirt gets in, and the joint wears. The classic sign is a clicking or popping noise when turning, or grease sprayed on the inside of the wheel.

How urgent is it?

Fix soon

A clicking joint usually gives you some warning time, but a joint that fails completely can leave the car unable to move. Loud clunking or vibration means get it looked at right away.

Questions to ask the shop

  • Is it the boot that's torn or is the joint itself worn?
  • Which side, and is it the inner or outer joint?
